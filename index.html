🎵 Baby Piano & Music World

Privacy Policy

Plain-language, and written specifically for a children's app.

1Who this app is for

Baby Piano & Music World is designed for young children. This policy is written to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar children's-privacy laws in other regions.

2Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account information — there is no sign-up or login
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • Any way for a child to chat, message, or share content publicly

3What's stored, and where

Game progress — stars earned, unlocked animals, character customization, saved songs, drawings, high scores, and sound settings — is saved only on your device, using local on-device storage. None of it is uploaded to a server, and we have no access to it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently.

4Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Every ad request is flagged to Google as coming from a child-directed app, which limits ads to non-personalized, contextual, general-audience content — not behavioral or interest-based advertising.

Google, as the ad-serving provider, may process technical identifiers (such as a device's advertising ID) to deliver and measure ads. This happens under Google's own privacy practices, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy and, specific to AdMob, Google's AdMob data disclosure.

We do not build our own advertising profiles and do not sell any data.

5Parental controls

Settings that affect purchases, ads, and app configuration are placed behind a simple parental gate (a short math problem) so a young child can't wander into them by accident. This is a usability safeguard, not a security login.

6Children's privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no age-gating beyond the parental gate is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in some unforeseen way, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

7Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date below will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing on the next update.
